# Technical demo script (3-5 minutes)

1. Open the control plane and show Studionet, chain `61999`, and the explicit
   wallet-disconnected state.
2. Create and seal a Mandate. Show the principal, agent, limits, evidence
   authorities, fingerprint, and immutable status.
3. Submit an Intent and show the frozen recipient, amount, deliverable, and
   evidence identity.
4. Capture evidence and show authority, transport, digest, byte length, and
   write-once snapshot identity.
5. Run authorization and show the bounded semantic vector. Explain that it
   cannot choose economic values.
6. Reserve GEN and show the Vault accounting transition, not a fake payment.
7. Show a raw submitted challenge beside an authenticated qualifying challenge.
8. Append fulfillment evidence and show the independent fulfillment vector.
9. Record the deterministic settlement direction, then show release pending
   until the external-message observation boundary is satisfied.
10. Open Proof and Security to show source provenance, network guard, and
    invariants; finish with the qualification history and known limitations.
